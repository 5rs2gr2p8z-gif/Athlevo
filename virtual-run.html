Two results

Finishing is one result. Sub-1 is another.

Finisher

Run a 10k and submit your result.

Any finishing time counts. You earn your certificate of completion, plus the finisher shirt and medal if your package includes them.

Sub-1

Run a 10k in under 60:00.

Your result is verified against the official event rules before it is recognised as Sub-1. Verified Sub-1 finishers also earn a Sub-1 certificate.

We cannot promise a sub-60 time. Your result depends on your fitness, your consistency and the day you run.

    Questions

    Before you register.

    When is the challenge?

    Dates to Be Announced. The challenge is 8 weeks long. Join the priority list and we will email you as soon as the dates are confirmed.

    Is this a live race?

    No. It is a virtual challenge. You train for eight weeks, then run your 10k yourself and submit your result.

    What if I have never run a 10k?

    You can still register. The challenge is built to take you from where you are. Share your current 5k and 10k times if you have them, and leave them blank if you do not.

    Is a sub-60 10k guaranteed?

    No. The plan gives you structure to chase it. Your result depends on your fitness, your consistency and the day you run.

    What is the difference between finishing and Sub-1?

    Finishing means you ran a 10k and submitted your result, at any time. Sub-1 means a 10k under 60:00 that has been verified against the official event rules.

    What is the difference between the packages?

    The Digital Challenge is your entry, the standard training guide, a digital bib, a certificate and the leaderboard. The Finisher Kit adds the performance shirt and finisher medal. The Performance Bundle adds a personalized 8-week plan built around your fitness, goals and availability.

    How does delivery work?

    Shipping is included within the stated delivery coverage. We collect your size and address when you register. Delivery coverage and timing are shown with the packages.

    What about refunds and changes?

    They are covered in the official event rules, which you agree to when you register.

    What if I have pain or an injury?

    See a doctor or physiotherapist before you start. We share general guidance for healthy runners and cannot diagnose anything.
