The approach behind Athlevo
The Athlevo Method.
Athlevo’s guidance is designed to account for more than a single workout. The runner’s history, goals, constraints, and feedback all matter when thinking through what comes next.

This is a training framework inside the product — not a claim of a proprietary scientific breakthrough, and not a replacement for a human coach.
FAQ

Straight answers.

What is Athlevo AI?

An AI training companion built specifically for runners. It is designed to help you discuss your training, understand context, and think through training decisions.

How does connected training history inform its answers?

When training data is available to your account, Athlevo can use relevant sessions and recent patterns as context for the conversation. The amount and type of history available depend on your account and supported integration.

Which wearables or platforms are supported?

For this preview, Garmin is verified in the current interface and Strava / Intervals.icu are present in the current integration architecture. Re-confirm public compatibility list

Can I use it without connecting a wearable?

Yes, Athlevo can work with information you provide directly, but connected training history gives it more context. Some features are naturally more useful when training data is available.

Does it create or adjust training plans?

The current implementation includes AI-backed training plan generation and adjustment flows. This landing-page preview deliberately leads with understanding and decision support rather than presenting automatic plan changes as the entire product.

How is my training data handled?

Athlevo uses account and training information to provide app functionality. AI-powered features may process relevant messages and training context through third-party AI services, including OpenAI, after the required consent flow. See the Privacy Policy for the complete current disclosure.

When will it be available, and what will it cost?

Version 1.0.0 for Android can be downloaded directly from this page. The iPhone version is coming soon. Review pricing before publication The current product has internal Free / Pro configuration, but public launch pricing should be confirmed before this page goes live.
