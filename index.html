Frequently asked questions

What does Athlevo include?
Athlevo includes an adaptive endurance training plan, daily training recommendations, readiness-based guidance, workout analysis, personalized training paces, athlete development tracking and AI coach conversations — all in one platform.
What can I use for free?
The free version includes onboarding, activity syncing, Today, readiness, your training calendar, basic Trends, one initial training plan and 3 Coach messages per week.
When will I be charged?
The free version does not require payment. You are charged only when you choose Athlevo Performance through Whop, at ₱597 per month.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. You can cancel Athlevo Performance at any time. You retain paid access until the end of your current billing period, then return to the free version.
Do I need a sports watch?
No. Athlevo works without a sports watch. You can log workouts manually. If you do have a compatible device, connecting it allows Athlevo to automatically sync your activity data for more detailed analysis.
How does activity syncing work?
Athlevo can sync completed workouts from compatible platforms such as Strava and other supported services. Once connected, your activities are imported automatically and analyzed as part of your training context.
Can I use Athlevo without connected activity data?
Yes. You can use Athlevo with manual workout logging and the daily readiness check. Connecting activity data adds richer analysis, but it is not required.
Which athletes is Athlevo currently best suited for?
Athlevo is currently best suited for runners training for distances from 5K to marathon, and athletes building general endurance. It can also account for strength and cross-training activity alongside running.
How is Athlevo different from a static plan or generic AI chatbot?
A static plan does not change when your life does. A generic AI chatbot answers one question at a time without remembering your history. Athlevo connects your goals, training history, readiness and completed workouts so every recommendation reflects your full context.
Is Athlevo medical or injury-treatment advice?
Athlevo provides training guidance and educational information. It does not diagnose injuries or replace medical care from a qualified professional.

Form = Fitness − Fatigue

The gray line is your Form over time. Its current value determines your Training Status.

Form shows the balance between long-term fitness and short-term fatigue.

Positive Form: fresher Near zero: balanced Negative Form: carrying fatigue

About Training Status
  • Detraining: very low recent load; fitness may begin declining.
  • Fresh: reduced fatigue; often suitable for racing or key sessions.
  • Maintaining: fitness and fatigue are relatively balanced.
  • Gaining Fitness: productive training load with manageable fatigue.
  • High Risk: fatigue is unusually high and recovery should be prioritized.

Why Threshold works — and why more isn't better

LT2 is your sustainable ceiling. Below it, lactate production and clearance stay in balance. Above it, the debt compounds and the session's recovery cost explodes.

The Norwegian insight: train slightly below LT2, in controlled repeats. You accumulate far more quality minutes per week than one heroic effort ever could — and you can repeat it Friday after Friday.

Your application: that's why your repeats are 4:52–4:58/km, not "as fast as possible." The pace ceiling is the workout.
